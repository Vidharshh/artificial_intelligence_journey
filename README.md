# 🚀 My AI Learning Journey

Welcome to my AI learning journey!

This repository is where I document my journey of learning Artificial Intelligence, 
starting from the fundamentals and gradually progressing towards Machine Learning, 
Deep Learning, Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Agentic AI.

Rather than just learning concepts theoretically, I aim to understand how they work, 
why they exist, where they are useful, and how to implement them through code.

This repository will serve as my personal knowledge base, learning journal, 
and collection of practical implementations as I explore the world of AI.

---

## 🎯 My Goal

My goal is to build a strong understanding of AI, from its foundational algorithms 
to modern intelligent systems.

I want to understand not just how to use AI tools, but also the concepts, 
architectures, algorithms, and engineering principles behind them.

## 💡 My Learning Philosophy

I believe that understanding the fundamentals makes it easier to understand 
the technologies built on top of them.

I want to move beyond knowing *what* an AI technology does and understand 
*how* it works, *why* it works, and *when* it should be used.

One concept at a time. One implementation at a time. One step closer to 
understanding AI.

**This is my journey from AI fundamentals to intelligent, agentic systems.**
